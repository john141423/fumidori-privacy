1Who we are

Fumidori ふみどり is an app for learning Japanese kanji, from level N5 to early N3, available in Spanish and English and distributed through Google Play.

The person responsible for the app and for this policy is Jonatan Ramos Pastrana, based in Japan.

For any privacy question, you can write to fumidori.app@gmail.com.

In this policy, “we” means the person responsible for Fumidori and “you” means the person using the app.

2Information we collect

None. Fumidori has no servers and no user accounts, and it does not send us any data. We do not know who uses the app or how they use it.

We have checked this by reviewing the app’s code and configuration: it contains no connections to servers and no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, sign-in or payment tools.

3Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your study progress: correct and wrong answers and review dates for each word and kanji.
  • Your streak and today’s activity.
  • The short readings you have completed and their results.
  • Your achievements.
  • Your settings: language, theme, furigana, chosen kanji and daily pace.
  • The study profile you give when you start: goal, JLPT level, exam date (only if you enter it), your class level, minutes per day and how much you already know.
  • The kanji you mark as seen in your class, if you study at a school.
  • The time of the daily reminder, if you turn it on.
  • The name you write for the progress image, if you write one.
  • Whether you have activated a promotional code.

This information does not leave your phone on the app’s initiative, and we have no access to it.

4Information we do not collect

Fumidori does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or address.
  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos, videos, files or audio recordings.
  • Device or advertising identifiers.
  • Your browsing history or data from other apps.
  • Payment information.
  • Usage statistics or its own crash reports.

The app uses no cookies or tracking technologies and shows no ads.

5How the information is used

The information stored on your phone is used only to make the app work: to show your progress, to decide which words to review and when, to remember your settings and, if you turn it on, to remind you every day at the time you choose.

It is not used for advertising, for profiling or for automated decisions that affect you.

6Internet connection

Fumidori works offline. The fonts, the kanji data and the stroke order data are included in the app.

Android shows that the app has the internet access permission because the framework it is built with (Capacitor) includes it by default, but Fumidori does not use it.

Some links (those in “Credits and licences” and the link to this policy) open in your browser, outside the app, only if you tap them. From then on, the policies of those websites apply.

7Phone features and third-party services

Fumidori does not use third-party services that receive your data. Some features use services on your own phone or services you choose:

  • Voice. When you tap the listen button, the app passes the Japanese word or sentence to be spoken to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example, Google’s or Samsung’s). It only receives that text from the app, never your data or your progress. That engine is not part of Fumidori and works according to its own settings and privacy policy.
  • Daily reminder. If you turn it on, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a reminder on your own phone. No notifications are sent from servers. You can turn it off in Options or in your phone’s settings.
  • Progress backup. If you export your progress to a file, the app creates it on your phone and opens the “Share” menu. You choose where to save it (Drive, Files, email…), and the policy of the service you choose applies. The backup code is only shown on your screen.
  • Progress image. If you create an image of your progress (in “My progress”), it is drawn on your phone and the “Share” menu opens. You choose whether to send it and to whom.
  • Teacher lists. If you paste a list of kanji your teacher sends you, the app reads that text on your phone to find the kanji. If you are a teacher and share a list, it is shared as an ordinary message from the app you choose.
  • Android backup. If it is turned on on your phone, Android may include the app’s data in the backup of your Google account. Google manages that backup according to your settings, and we have no access to it.
  • Google Play. Google handles the download, installation and updates. Google may provide us with aggregated statistics (for example, number of installs) and, if you allow it in your phone’s settings, technical crash reports. Google processes that data under its own privacy policy; it does not include your progress or your name.

8Retention

Data stored on your phone is kept until you delete it, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. We keep no data about you, because we never receive it.

Backup files you export are kept wherever you save them, for as long as you decide.

9Security

The app’s data is kept in its private storage, which Android protects so that other apps cannot read it. As the app does not send data over the internet, there are no transmissions to protect and no servers that could be accessed without authorisation.

We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock and keeping any backup files you export in a safe place, because they contain your study progress.

10Your rights

Data protection law (for example, the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation or Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information) gives you the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.

As we hold no data about you, there is no data we can view, correct or delete on your behalf. You control all the information from the app: you can see it in “My progress”, export it to a file (portability) and delete it whenever you want.

If you have any question, write to fumidori.app@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

11How to delete your data

You can delete all of Fumidori’s data in any of these ways:

  1. In the app: Options → “Reset my progress”.
  2. In Android: Settings → Apps → Fumidori → Storage → “Clear data”.
  3. By uninstalling the app.

To turn off the reminder, go to Options → “Daily reminder” or to your phone’s notification settings. Android backup is managed from your Google account settings.

You do not need to ask us to delete anything, because we keep no data about you. If you still want to ask us something, write to fumidori.app@gmail.com.

12Children

Fumidori is intended for people aged 18 and over. Its content is educational and suitable for all ages, and the app collects no data from anyone, including children.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any question, write to fumidori.app@gmail.com.

13International transfers

We do not transfer data to other countries, because we receive none. The services involved by your choice or your phone’s (Google Play, Android backup, the text-to-speech engine or the service you choose when sharing a file) may process data in other countries under their own policies.

14Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date. If the change is important (for example, if the app started collecting any data), we will also say so in the app before it applies.

15Contact

For any question about privacy or this policy: fumidori.app@gmail.com.
