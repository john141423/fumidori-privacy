1Controller

The controller of Fumidori and of this privacy policy is Jonatan Ramos Pastrana, an independent developer based in Japan who publishes the app under the name “Fumidori”.

  • Email: fumidori.app@gmail.com
  • App: Fumidori ふみどり, distributed through Google Play.

In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the controller, and “you” refers to the person who uses the app or visits its websites.

2Scope of this policy

This policy applies to:

  • The Fumidori app for Android.
  • Fumidori’s official websites: the app’s presentation page and this legal information page, both hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Any communication you send us by email.

It does not apply to third-party services you use together with the app (for example, Google Play, your device’s text-to-speech engine or the service you choose to save or share a file), which are governed by their own policies.

3General principle: the app does not collect personal data

Fumidori is designed to work without collecting personal data. It has no servers, does not require an account and does not send us any information about you or about how you use it. We do not know who uses the app.

We have verified this by reviewing the app’s code and configuration: it contains no connections to our own or third-party servers, and no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, sign-in or payment tools.

4Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following information in its private storage on your device:

  • Your study progress: correct and incorrect answers, stage and review date for each word and kanji.
  • Your activity: streak, streak shields, days studied and answers per day.
  • The short readings you have completed and your achievements.
  • Your settings: language, appearance, furigana, sounds, daily pace, the kanji or levels you have chosen, and the words and sentences shown recently (so they are not repeated).
  • The study profile you enter at the start: goal, level, exam date (if you enter it), class level and minutes per day.
  • The kanji you add to “My list” or mark as seen in class.
  • The time of the daily reminder, if you turn it on.
  • The name you type for the progress image, if you type one.
  • Whether you have activated a promo code.

This information never leaves your device on the app’s initiative, and we have no access to it. Because we do not receive it, we do not process it within the meaning of data protection law: it remains under your exclusive control.

5Data we process if you email us

If you send us an email, we receive your email address, your name (if it appears in the message) and the content of your message.

  • Purpose: to answer your question, consider your suggestion or fix the error you report.
  • Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in handling it (Article 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR) and, where applicable, Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI).
  • Retention: for as long as needed to handle your request and no longer than twelve months after our last exchange, unless a legal obligation requires us to keep it longer.
  • Provider: email is handled through Gmail, a Google service that operates under its own terms and privacy policy.

We do not use this data for any other purpose, we do not share it with third parties and we will not send you marketing messages.

6Information we do not collect

Fumidori does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos, videos, files or audio recordings.
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
  • Your browsing history or data from other apps.
  • Payment information.
  • Usage statistics or crash reports of its own.

The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies and does not show ads.

7App permissions

Fumidori requests only the Android permissions its features need:

  • Show notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the daily reminder, and only if you turn it on. The app asks for the permission at that moment.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) and prevent the device from sleeping (WAKE_LOCK): so the reminder keeps working after you restart your phone.
  • Internet access (INTERNET): included by default by the framework the app is built with (Capacitor). Fumidori does not use it; it works completely offline.

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

8Device features and third-party services

Fumidori does not engage any third-party services that receive your data. Some features use services on your own device or services you choose:

  • Voice. When you tap the listen button, the app passes the Japanese word or sentence to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example, Google’s or Samsung’s). It receives only that text, never your data or your progress. The engine works according to its own settings and privacy policy.
  • Daily reminder. It is scheduled on your own device; no notifications are sent from servers. You can turn it off in Options or in your Android settings.
  • Progress backup. If you export your progress, the app creates a file on your device and opens the “Share” menu. You decide where to save it (for example, Google Drive or your email), and that service’s policy applies.
  • Progress image and teacher lists. They are created and read on your device and are shared only if you choose to, through the app you pick.
  • Android backup. If you have it turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your Google account backup. Google manages it according to your settings, and we have no access to it.
  • Google Play. Google handles downloads, installation and updates. It may provide us with aggregated statistics (such as the number of installs) and, if you allow it in your device settings, technical crash reports. Google processes that data under its own privacy policy; it does not include your progress or your name.

9Websites

Fumidori’s websites are static: they use no cookies, forms, analytics or tracking tools, and they load no resources from other domains. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, for security and operational purposes, under its own privacy policy. We have no access to those logs.

Links in the app (for example, in “Credits and licenses” or to these legal texts) open in your browser only if you tap them.

10Retention

Information stored on your device is kept until you delete it, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. Backup files you export are kept wherever you save them, for as long as you decide.

Emails you send us are kept as described in section 5.

11Security

The app’s data is stored in its private storage, which Android protects from access by other apps. Because the app does not transmit data, there are no transmissions to intercept and no servers that could be breached.

We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping any backup files you export in a safe place, as they contain your study progress.

12Your rights

Applicable data protection law (for example, the GDPR in the European Union or the APPI in Japan) may give you the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw your consent.

  • App data: you control it directly. You can view it in “My progress”, export it to a file and delete it at any time (section 13). Because we do not receive it, we cannot view, correct or delete it on your behalf.
  • Your emails: you can exercise your rights by writing to fumidori.app@gmail.com. We will reply within the period required by applicable law.

If you believe we have not handled your rights properly, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country; for example, the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) in Spain or the Personal Information Protection Commission (ppc.go.jp) in Japan.

13How to delete your data

You can delete all of Fumidori’s data in any of these ways:

  1. In the app: Options → “Reset my progress”.
  2. In Android: Settings → Apps → Fumidori → Storage → “Clear data”.
  3. By uninstalling the app.

You can turn off the reminder in Options → “Daily reminder” or in your Android notification settings. Android backup is managed from your Google account settings. To have us delete an email you sent us, write to fumidori.app@gmail.com.

14Children

Fumidori is intended for people aged 18 and over. Its content is educational and suitable for all ages, and the app does not collect data from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please write to us.

15International transfers

The app does not transfer data, because it does not collect any. Emails you send us are handled through Gmail and may be stored on Google servers outside your country; Google applies the safeguards described in its privacy policy and required by applicable law. Services involved by your choice or by your device settings (Google Play, Android backup, the text-to-speech engine or the service you choose when sharing a file) may process data in other countries under their own policies.

16Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or in the law. We will publish the new version on this page with its update date. If the change is significant (for example, if the app started collecting any data), we will also announce it in the app before it takes effect.

17Contact

For any question about privacy or this policy, write to fumidori.app@gmail.com.
