3Description of the service

Fumidori is an educational tool for learning Japanese kanji, from level N5 to early N3 of the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT), available in Spanish and English. It includes flashcards, exercises, scheduled reviews, handwriting practice, short readings and other study features.

The app works offline, requires no registration and stores all information on your device.
